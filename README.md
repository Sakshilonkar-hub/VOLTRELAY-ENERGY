# VOLTRELAY-ENERGY
VoltRelay’s battery-swapping network expanded substantially between January 2024 and June 2025. Monthly completed swaps increased from 103,508 to 303,196, while monthly revenue rose from ₹6.20 million to ₹19.69 million. However, network growth was accompanied by a deterioration in reliability: the monthly failure rate increased from 4.4% to 8.6%. 
# VoltRelay – Business Performance & Root-Cause Analysis

## Project Overview
Analyzed VoltRelay's battery-swapping network performance using Python and Google Colab to identify operational challenges, revenue trends, service failures, battery performance issues, customer churn, and profitability gaps.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Google Colab
- Data Analysis and Visualization

## Key Areas of Analysis
1. Network Performance and Revenue Trends
2. Service Failures and Customer Experience
3. Station and Charger Performance
4. Battery Supplier and Manufacturing Lot Analysis
5. Pricing and Fleet Partner Economics
6. New-Rider Retention and Churn

## Key Findings
- Monthly completed swaps increased by approximately 193%.
- Monthly revenue increased by approximately 218%.
- Network failure rate increased from 4.4% to 8.6%.
- Gen1 chargers recorded a 7.23% failure rate.
- Kyron batteries showed significantly higher degradation.
- Early service failures were associated with higher customer churn.

## Recommendations
- Improve battery availability and charger reliability.
- Investigate underperforming battery manufacturing lots.
- Review loss-making fleet partner contracts.
- Improve new-rider onboarding and retention.
